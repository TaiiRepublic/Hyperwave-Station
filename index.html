Hyperwave Chat

Every platform's chat in one overlay. Set it up here, then paste the link into OBS.

Made by TaiiRepublic. Free for any streamer to use.

Starting style

Applies the look, display mode and a matching chat position. Your channels and filters stay as they are.

How to set this up
  1. Twitch works right away. Under Chat sources, leave Twitch on Direct and type your channel name.
  2. YouTube (and optionally Kick) needs Streamer.bot. Connect your accounts there, then open Servers/Clients → WebSocket Server, set Port to 8080, tick Auto Start and click Start. Set those platforms to "Through Streamer.bot" here.
  3. TikTok needs TikFinity open and connected to your LIVE. Set TikTok to "Through TikFinity".
  4. Pick a starting style and display mode, adjust anything you like, and watch the preview. Send test message adds sample chat.
  5. Pick your stream resolution under Screen and position, then drag the dashed chat box in the preview to where chat should sit on your screen. Drag its corners to resize it.
  6. Click Copy link. In OBS, add a Browser source, paste it into URL, and set Width and Height to your stream resolution (1920 and 1080 by default). Then right-click the source and choose Transform → Fit to screen.
  7. Changed something later? Copy the new link into the same OBS source, then right-click it and choose Refresh.

Your settings live only in your link and this browser. Nothing is sent to anyone else.

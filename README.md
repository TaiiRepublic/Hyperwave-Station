# Hyperwave Chat

Every platform's chat in one OBS overlay. Free for any streamer to use. Made by [TaiiRepublic](https://twitch.tv/taiirepublic).

**Open the app:** https://TaiiRepublic.github.io/hyperwave-chat/

## What it does
- Twitch with nothing to install, plus Kick, YouTube (through Streamer.bot) and TikTok (through TikFinity)
- Six ways to show chat: classic list, Matrix rain, float up, scroll left to right, scroll right to left, and pop-up scatter
- Optional digital rain background behind your chat
- 20 entrance animations and 8 exit animations
- Drag the chat anywhere on screen and resize it, at 1080p, 720p, 1440p, 4K, ultrawide or vertical resolutions
- Boxes, pills, speech bubbles and sci-fi panels, colored by chatter, platform, or your own palette
- Profile pictures with initials as a fallback
- Twitch, 7TV, BetterTTV, FrankerFaceZ and Kick emotes, badges and emoji
- 24 fonts, outlines, glow, filters, highlights and eleven starting styles, all with a live preview

## Setup
1. Open the app link above and follow "How to set this up" in the left panel.
2. Pick a style, click **Copy link**, and paste it into an OBS **Browser** source.

Your settings are stored only in your own overlay link and browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
